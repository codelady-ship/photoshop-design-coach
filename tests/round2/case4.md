Works: Bold yellow is warm, eye-catching and fits a coffee shop opening.

Fix first:
1. Headline: white #FFFFFF on #FFD400 → change to black #000000 or #1A1A1A. White on this yellow is about 1.43:1 (needs 3:1 for large text), so it nearly disappears. Black gives about 14.7:1, #1A1A1A about 12.2:1.
2. Body text: #777777 on #FFD400 → change to #1A1A1A. Now it is about 3.13:1, but normal text needs 4.5:1.
3. Body size: 14 px is too small for both uses. On A3 at 300 ppi it is only about 1.2 mm tall. On a 1080 px Instagram post, body should be at least about 28-32 px, and the headline much bigger than 60 px. Set the size per file (see the format note).
4. Fonts: 4 families → keep 2, one for the headline and one for the text. Use bold/regular weights for variety.
5. Focal point and key info: make the headline the one main thing. Put the date, place and opening time right under it, big enough to find in 2 seconds. I can't see the poster, so check this one yourself.
Logo: fine in the corner (logos are exempt from contrast). Keep it at least 3 mm inside the trim on the A3 (5 mm is safer).

Format fit: this needs two separate files. A3 print is 297 x 420 mm + 3 mm bleed = 303 x 426 mm (3579 x 5031 px at 300 ppi), and the yellow must run to the bleed edge. Instagram is 1080 x 1350 px (4:5), RGB. The print shop's own rules win.
