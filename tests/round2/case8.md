Goal: a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail with your cut-out photo and big text, made in Photopea (free, in the browser).

Size: 1280 × 720 px (16:9). YouTube now recommends 3840 × 2160, same ratio. Minimum width 640 px, JPG or PNG, under 2 MB to be safe.

1. New canvas — `File ▸ New` ▸ Width 1280 px, Height 720 px (RGB). Fill the background with a bright, simple colour.
2. Open your photo — `File ▸ Open`. Pick a photo where your face is large and well lit.
3. Cut it out — `Select ▸ Remove BG`. Check the edges, hair especially.
4. Fix rough edges without erasing — `Layer ▸ Raster Mask ▸ Add` (or the mask icon at the bottom of the Layers panel). Paint black to hide, white to show (`X` swaps colours).
5. Move it to the thumbnail — select all (`Ctrl+A` / `Cmd+A`), copy (`Ctrl+C` / `Cmd+C`), switch to your 1280 × 720 tab, paste (`Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V`). Scale with `Alt+Ctrl+T` (on Mac try `Option+Cmd+T`; plain `Ctrl+T` opens a browser tab). Put yourself on one side, face large.
6. Add text — Type tool `T`, 3 to 4 words max, a bold font, very large, on the other side.
7. Make the text readable — `Layer ▸ Layer Style ▸ Stroke` (2–6 px, dark) or `Drop Shadow`; double-clicking the layer also works. Check the menu, names can differ slightly.
8. Export — `File ▸ Export As ▸ JPG` (quality 80–90 %).

Check: zoom out until the image is about thumbnail size (small). You should still read the words and see your face.

Common mistake: too many words in small letters, or text over a busy area. Keep key text away from the bottom-right corner, where the video time stamp sits (that placement is general practice, not from YouTube's size page).
