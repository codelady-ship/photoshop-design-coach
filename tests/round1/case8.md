Goal: YouTube thumbnail in Photopea with your photo cut out and big text.

Size: 1280 x 720 px (16:9) is the classic size. YouTube now recommends 3840 x 2160 px, same 16:9 shape. Minimum width is 640 px. Use JPG or PNG. Max file size is 2 MB on mobile and 50 MB on desktop. Screen only needs pixels, so ppi does not matter. Use RGB.

1. New document: open photopea.com ▸ File ▸ New ▸ Width 1280 px, Height 720 px (or 3840 x 2160 px), RGB. Menu names may differ slightly, so check the menu.
2. Add your photo: File ▸ Open (or drag the photo into the window). Select the photo layer.
3. Cut out: Select ▸ Remove BG. Photopea removes the background in one click. Check the edges, especially hair.
4. Move the cut-out into your thumbnail: copy the cut-out layer and paste it into the 1280 x 720 document. Then use Free Transform, `Alt+Ctrl+T` (Edit ▸ Free Transform), to scale it. Hold `Shift` if it stretches. Press `Enter` to apply.
5. Background: add a simple bright or contrasting colour or image on a layer under your cut-out. A flat colour is fine.
6. Big text: Type tool `T`. Use 3 to 4 words at most, a thick bold font, around 150 px or more. Put the text on the opposite side from your face, with white or yellow text on a dark background (or the reverse). Keep it away from the bottom-right corner, because the video time stamp covers it. Check that it is readable when the thumbnail is tiny.
7. Export: File ▸ Export As ▸ JPG (quality 80 to 90 %) or PNG. Check that the file is under 2 MB, then upload.

Check: shrink the preview to about the size of a phone thumbnail. If you can still read the text and see your face, it works.

Common mistake: small text or too many words. A thumbnail is seen very small, so keep it to one idea.

Faster: if Select ▸ Remove BG leaves rough edges, add a Layer ▸ Raster Mask ▸ Add and paint with black to hide leftovers and white to bring parts back.

Free options: Canva Free also has 16:9 templates, but its Background Remover is Pro only. remove.bg is free only as a small preview.
