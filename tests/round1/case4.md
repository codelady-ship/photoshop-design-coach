Works: the bright yellow #FFD400 is warm and eye-catching, a good fit for a coffee shop opening.

Fix first:
1. White headline on yellow → switch it to near-black `#1A1A1A` (white on #FFD400 is only about 1.43:1, it fails even the 3:1 minimum for large text; #1A1A1A on yellow is about 12.2:1).
2. Grey body #777777 on yellow → also `#1A1A1A` (it is about 3.13:1, passes only for large text; 14 px body needs 4.5:1).
3. 14 px body is too small → on a 1080 px Instagram post use at least 28-32 px. For A3, a pixel size means nothing until we know the canvas: the full A3 file is 3579 × 5031 px, where 60 px is only about 5 mm tall. Set sizes in pt/mm for print (headline 70 pt or more, body 18-24 pt, readable from 1-2 m).
4. 4 fonts → keep 2 families: one bold for the headline, one clean for the text (more fonts look messy and hurt hierarchy). Make sure they support ə ı ş ğ if you use Azerbaijani.
5. Logo in the corner → keep it, logos are exempt from contrast rules, but place it at least 3 mm inside the trim for print, and for Instagram keep it inside the margins (60-80 px on a 1080 post).

Two files, not one. They need different sizes:
- Print A3: 297 × 420 mm + 3 mm bleed = 303 × 426 mm → 3579 × 5031 px at 300 ppi. The yellow must run to the bleed edge. Ask the print shop about CMYK, PDF and fonts first; their rules win. Bright yellow can shift in CMYK, so soft-proof it (`View ▸ Proof Colors`).
- Instagram: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5), RGB. Make it a separate version, not a shrunk A3.
