Works: the bright #FFD400 yellow is warm and eye-catching for a coffee shop opening, and your headline is clearly bigger than the body (about 4×).

2. Body: grey #777777 on yellow is about 3.13:1 → use #1A1A1A too. Normal text needs 4.5:1, so grey fails. Keep the yellow, change the text.
3. Fonts: 4 families → keep 2 (one for the headline, one for the body). Four fonts make the poster look messy and hide the main message.
4. Text size: 14 px body is too small for Instagram, where body should be about 28-32 px on a 1080 px wide post. Tell me your document size and I will check the headline for A3 too, because 60 px is tiny on a 3579 px wide A3 file.
