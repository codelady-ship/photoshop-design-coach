# Junior designer job and test-task prep

Be honest: no promise of a job, no fake experience, certificates or client names. Never put client work in a portfolio without permission; label self-made briefs as "concept / self-initiated".

## What Baku employers ask for (hellojob.az, 8 design vacancies opened 2026-11-11)
- Photoshop + Illustrator almost everywhere; After Effects/Premiere (motion) more and more; Canva and AI tools (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Firefly, Runway…) often listed; Figma at banks/tech.
- Work: social posts and stories, ads, web banners, print flyers/posters/catalogues, resizing one design for many platforms, handing over editable source files.
- Portfolio: almost always required ("applications without a portfolio are not considered" in one ad).
- Pay seen for junior/1–2 years: about 700–1,200 AZN/month; SMM roles 300–700 AZN. Snapshot only; varies.
- Some ads ask for Russian. CorelDRAW did not appear in the ads opened.
Say: "Learn Photoshop well first, then Illustrator basics, then simple motion. Show AI use honestly (what you did by hand)."

## Portfolio (6–10 pieces beats 30)
Each piece: the brief (1 line), your process (sketch → versions → final), the result in the real format (mockup), and what you'd improve.
Mix:
1. Social media set for one brand: 3 feed posts + 2 stories + 1 carousel, same template (shows consistency).
2. Resize set: one campaign in 1080 × 1350, 1080 × 1920, 1280 × 720 (YouTube), web banner.
3. Print piece with bleed: flyer A5 or business card (show the PDF settings).
4. Original logo/identity for a made-up or real local small business (with permission), not a copy of a known brand.
5. Photo retouch / cut-out before–after.
6. Self-initiated project you care about (poster series, event, cause).
Where: Behance (free), a PDF of 8–12 pages under 10 MB, Instagram @ for design only. Name files `Name_Surname_Portfolio_2026.pdf`.

## Test task (often 24–72 h, or 1–3 h in office)
Typical: a social post + story set for their brand; resize one design to 3–4 formats; a flyer with bleed; a banner from a tight brief; a logo refresh.
Time plan for a 3-hour task:
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Read the brief twice; list sizes, text, deadline, file format; ask 1–2 questions if allowed |
| 0:15–0:45 | 2–3 quick sketches/layouts; pick one |
| 0:45–2:15 | Build it: correct size first, guides/safe zone, named layers, groups |
| 2:15–2:40 | Variants/resizes; check contrast and spelling |
| 2:40–3:00 | Export (PNG/JPG for screen, PDF with bleed for print) + PSD; send early |

Self-check before sending:
- Sizes exactly as the brief; ratio right; text inside safe zones.
- Layers named and grouped; no hidden junk layers.
- Fonts: names listed or links sent (never send Adobe Fonts files).
- Images licensed (Unsplash/Pexels/Adobe Stock or their assets).
- Exports at 100 % look sharp; file names clear (`brand_post_1080x1350_v1.png`).
- Sent before the deadline with a 3-line note: idea, fonts, files.

## Likely interview questions (one-line model answers; use your own examples)
1. **Walk me through a project.** Brief → research/moodboard → sketches → 2–3 versions → feedback → final → export.
2. **RGB vs CMYK, and ppi?** RGB for screens, CMYK for print; 300 ppi at final size for print, pixels only matter for screen.
3. **How do you prepare a file for print?** Final size + 3 mm bleed, safe zone, CMYK/profile the shop asks for, PDF/X, fonts embedded.
4. **How do you handle feedback/revisions?** Ask what goal the change serves, keep versions (v1, v2), confirm in writing.
5. **How do you keep files organised?** Named layers, groups, smart objects, a folder per project, versioned names.
6. **How do you use AI tools?** For ideas, backgrounds or clean-up; I check rights and quality and do the final design myself.
7. **Fonts and photos: how do you check licences?** Google Fonts/Adobe Fonts/licensed stock; read the licence for commercial use.
8. **Why our company?** Mention their real posts/brand and one idea you'd bring.

## Certificates and learning
- Adobe Certified Professional exists (check the price and test centres on Adobe's site).
- Free learning: Adobe's own Photoshop tutorials (in-app Learn panel / helpx), GIMP docs, Photopea "learn" pages.
- Practice plan: one brief a day for 30 days (fake brands), post the best on Behance.
